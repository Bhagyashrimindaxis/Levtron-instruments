About Levtron Instruments

Leading the Industry in Precision Level Measurement

Levtron Instruments Pvt Ltd is committed to designing and manufacturing high-precision level detection systems. Our product suite includes Compact Vibrating Fork Switches, RF Admittance Switches, Rotating Paddles, Optical Sensors, and Digital Level Transmitters.

Whether monitoring corrosive liquids, fine powders, or heavy mining granules, Levtron instruments ensure zero-maintenance reliability and continuous process uptime.

Our Mission

Deliver zero-defect, high-precision level sensing instruments that maximize plant safety and operational efficiency.

Our Vision

To be recognized globally as the gold standard in industrial level switch and transmitter manufacturing.
