About Levtron Instruments

Leading the Way in Level Measurement Solutions

Levtron is committed to providing high-quality level measurement solutions, delivering precision and reliability through our advanced product offerings.

At Levtron, we specialize in state-of-the-art level measurement technology. Our extensive product range includes compact vibrating fork level switches, RF admittance level switches (rod and disc types), compact vibrating rod level switches, conductivity level switches, and miniature vibrating fork level switches. Each product is designed to meet the highest standards of accuracy and durability, ensuring optimal performance in various industrial applications. Our dedication to innovation and customer satisfaction positions us as a trusted partner in level measurement solutions.
